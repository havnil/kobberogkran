Prices

At Kobber og Kran you can see the prices before you get in touch. All prices include VAT, and you are told which kind of price applies before Eirik starts. The figures are further down the page.

Three ways to price a job

We use the same terms as the Norwegian Craftsman Services Act (håndverkertjenesteloven), so you know what you have agreed.

Hourly rate

Most service and repair jobs are charged at the hourly rate (timepris). You pay for the time the job takes, plus the service van, consumables and parts. Time is counted from when Eirik arrives until the job is done. He works efficiently and tells you along the way if it will take much longer than expected.

Estimate

If the job is bigger but it is hard to know exactly what it will take, you get an estimate (prisoverslag). It is a forecast of what the job will cost. Under the Craftsman Services Act an estimate may not be exceeded significantly. In practice, around 15% above the estimate is regarded as the limit. If Eirik sees it may cost more, he tells you before carrying on.

Fixed price

A fixed price (fastpris) means the price is agreed and does not change, even if the job takes longer. We have a fixed price for the work when a water heater is replaced in the same place, and can give fixed prices on other jobs once we know enough about them. It is always stated what the fixed price covers, and what is not included.

What you pay for

  • Service van per visit: covers the van, the tools and driving within our area. You pay it once per visit, so it pays to group small jobs together.
  • Consumables: sealants, tape, screws and other small materials used on most jobs.
  • Parts: taps, valves, pipes and fittings are charged as used. You can ask to know the price of more expensive parts before they are ordered.

Evenings and weekends

Kobber og Kran does not have a 24-hour emergency service. Work in the evenings and at weekends is by arrangement, and the price is agreed in advance.

Bathrooms

There is no set price for bathrooms, because no two are alike. You get a written offer after a site visit. Read more on the bathroom page.

Businesses, housing co-ops and owners’ associations

Businesses get an invoice with VAT shown separately. For housing co-ops, owners’ associations and builders we can make separate agreements. Get in touch.

Included in the fixed price

  • Draining and disconnecting the old tank
  • Fitting the new tank in the same place
  • Fitting the safety valve and mixing valve that go with the tank
  • Connecting the water and pressure testing
  • Taking the old tank away
  • Documentation (FDV) for what is installed

Not included in the fixed price

  • The tank itself and parts (charged on top)
  • Moving the tank to a different place
  • A new floor drain or leak protection to current standards
  • An electrician, if the tank is to be hard-wired
  • Difficult access, such as a cramped cellar or crawl space
