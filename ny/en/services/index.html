What do you need help with?

    A sink, shower drain or toilet that won't drain away. Eirik clears the drain and finds out why it keeps blocking.

    Brown water, a smell or low pressure

    Water off before the frost and back on in spring, done properly so the pipes survive the winter. Eirik also helps when something has frozen.

    See more →
  8. 08
