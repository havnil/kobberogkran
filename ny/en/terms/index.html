Terms

Terms of Kobber og Kran AS: service van and minimum charge, parts you have bought yourself, payment, site visits, cancellation and complaints.

Send requestCall000 00 000

These terms apply when Kobber og Kran AS does plumbing work for you. If you are a consumer, the Norwegian Craftsman Services Act (håndverkertjenesteloven) applies, and nothing here gives you fewer rights than the law. For businesses, housing co-ops and owners’ associations, these terms apply unless otherwise agreed in writing.

Price

We use the terms of the Craftsman Services Act: hourly rate (timepris), estimate (prisoverslag) and fixed price (fastpris). You are told which applies before work starts. All prices to consumers include VAT. See the prices page for current prices.

  • Hourly rate: you pay for the time spent, plus the service van, consumables and parts.
  • Estimate: a forecast that may not be exceeded significantly. In practice, around 15% is regarded as the limit. If we see it may cost more, we tell you before carrying on.
  • Fixed price: the price is agreed and does not change. It is always stated what the fixed price covers.

Service van and minimum charge

The service van is charged once per visit. It covers the van, the tools and driving within our area. We charge at least one hour of work per visit, even when the job itself is quicker. If you group several small jobs on the same visit, you only pay for the service van once.

Parts you have bought yourself

You can ask us to fit parts or equipment you have bought yourself. In that case:

  • We are responsible for our work, but not for faults or defects in the part itself. Take that up with the shop you bought it from.
  • If the part doesn’t fit, something is missing, or it is damaged, you pay for the time it takes and any extra parts.
  • We may decline to fit parts that are not approved for use in Norway, or that we consider unsafe.

Additional work

If it turns out along the way that the job needs more than agreed, we tell you and agree it with you before doing it. The exception is work that must be done at once to prevent damage, such as stopping a leak. We then let you know as soon as we can.

Site visits

Site visits before bathrooms and larger jobs are free within our area. If you want a site visit for a smaller job, or outside the area, it is charged at the hourly rate plus the service van. You are told before we come. Photos in the form are often enough.

Payment

  • We send an invoice when the job is done. Payment is due within 14 days.
  • For larger jobs we can agree on staged payments.
  • Bathrooms: materials ordered specially for your bathroom are paid up front or along the way, as agreed. This is always stated in the written offer.
  • All jobs are invoiced. We do not accept cash without an invoice.

Cancellation

You can cancel or move an agreed job free of charge up to 24 hours before the agreed time. If you cancel later, or nobody is home and there is no access when we arrive, we may charge for the service van. Materials ordered specially for the job must be paid for. The Craftsman Services Act gives you the right to cancel, but you may then have to cover work already done and costs we have incurred.

Complaints

If you are not happy with the work, tell us within a reasonable time after you discovered the fault. Under the Craftsman Services Act you can complain for up to five years after the work was completed for work on a home or cabin. We put right faults we are responsible for at no extra cost to you.

Disagreements

If we can’t agree, you as a consumer can get help from the Norwegian Consumer Council (Forbrukerrådet), and the case can be heard by the Consumer Complaints Committee (Forbrukerklageutvalget).
