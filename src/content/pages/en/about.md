Kobber og Kran AS is a new plumbing firm in Hemnes, Aurskog-Høland. Eirik owns the firm and is in charge of the work. When you call Kobber og Kran, you talk to the plumber who does the job, and we are at your door at the agreed time.

## The village's own plumber

Eirik started Kobber og Kran to be the plumber people around here can call. Not a call centre, but a local firm that knows the village and the houses, the wells and the cabins, and knows what a cold January does to a pipe.

## How we work

- **A price before we start.** You are told whether the job is at the hourly rate, an estimate or a fixed price before we begin.
- **Turns up when agreed.** We don't promise times we can't keep. If there is a delay, you are told.
- **Tidy.** Shoe covers, protecting the floor and cleaning up are part of the job.
- **Honest.** If there is something we don't do, such as drain jetting, electrical work or well drilling, we say so and help you on your way.
- **Documented.** You get documentation of what was done, for your home records and your insurer.

## Approvals

Kobber og Kran is newly started. We only show approvals we actually have, and they appear on this page once they are in place.

## Closer than you think

Kobber og Kran is based in Hemnes and works throughout Aurskog-Høland, in Indre Østfold and in Lillestrøm. See where we go on the areas page.
