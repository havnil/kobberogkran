Kobber og Kran is based in Hemnes, and most jobs are in Aurskog-Høland. A short drive means Eirik spends his time on the job, not in the van.

## All of Aurskog-Høland

We take jobs throughout the municipality: in Hemnes and Løken, in Bjørkelangen and Aurskog, and out towards Setskog and Rømskog. Many people here have their own well and a private drain, and that is everyday work for Eirik.

## Cabins in the forest

There are many cabins in the area, and many of them have no ordinary address. Use “Use where I am now” in the form while you are at the cabin, or give the farm and title number. Mention whether the road is cleared and where the key is.

## Lillestrøm

We also take jobs in Lillestrøm, preferably planned work such as replacing a water heater or bathroom plumbing.

## Outside the area

Outside the area we only take larger jobs, such as bathrooms or bigger work on wells and water treatment. The travel is then agreed in advance. Send a request and you'll get an honest answer about whether it works.
