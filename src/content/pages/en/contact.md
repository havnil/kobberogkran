The quickest way to reach Eirik is to call. If he is in the middle of a job and can't answer, you get a message with a link to the form, so you can send a photo and a description while you wait.

- **Call** if it's urgent, or if you'd rather talk to someone.
- **Write on WhatsApp** if that suits you best. You can send photos straight in the chat.
- **Send a request** with the form below. It takes half a minute, and you can add photos afterwards.

Leaking right now? Turn off the main stopcock first, switch off the power to the water heater and call. Kobber og Kran does not have a 24-hour emergency service. If Eirik can't answer, your insurer's emergency service is the quickest help.
