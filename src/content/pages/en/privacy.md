Kobber og Kran AS is responsible for the information you give us. We only collect what we need to help you, we don't pass anything on for sale, and the website has no tracking.

## What we store when you send a request

- **What it's about and how urgent it is**
- **Where the job is:** the address, the description of the place or the farm and title number you give. If you tap “Use where I am now”, the location (coordinates) is stored. We work out the approximate driving time from Hemnes.
- **Name and phone number**, and e-mail if you give it
- **Business name and organisation number** if you are ordering for a business
- **The description, answers and photos** you send, such as whether you have a well, the year of construction and access
- **How you heard about us**, if you answer, and which link you came from (for example a QR code on the van)
- **Your consents:** whether you want replies on WhatsApp, and whether you want service reminders

The purpose is to assess the job, give you a price, agree a time and do the work. The legal basis is that you are asking us to provide a service (GDPR Article 6(1)(b)).

## Photos

With the form, photos are made smaller in your browser before they are sent, and metadata, including the location from your phone camera, is removed. The server also removes such data from JPEG and PNG images before storing them. If you send a HEIC or WebP image from the page for sending more photos later, with JavaScript switched off, the image may be stored unchanged, including any location. Photos are only shown to Eirik, through links that expire.

## AI sorting

When a request comes in, an AI tool (Claude from Anthropic) writes a short summary for Eirik, so he can quickly see what is urgent and what is missing. The AI never writes to you, and Eirik reads everything himself.

- **Sent to the AI service:** what it's about, how urgent it is, your description and answers, what kind of photos you sent (not the photos themselves), the approximate driving time and whether the job is within our area. Phone numbers and e-mail addresses in the description are removed first.
- **Not sent:** your name, phone number, e-mail, the address or farm and title number, your location or the photos.

If you write an address in the description, it is included. Anthropic is our data processor and is based in the USA. The transfer is made with the safeguards GDPR requires, such as the EU Standard Contractual Clauses.

## Where the information is stored

The website, the requests and the photos are hosted by Cloudflare, and requests and photos are stored in data centres in Europe. Eirik is notified of new requests by e-mail.

Others who process information for us:

- **Kartverket (the Norwegian Mapping Authority):** when you type in the address field, what you type is sent there to show address suggestions. If you use “Use where I am now”, your location is sent there to find the address.
- **Cloudflare Turnstile:** checks that the form is sent by a person and not a robot. It only loads on pages with the form.
- **Messaging services:** the receipt, and the message if you called without getting an answer, are sent on WhatsApp (Meta), RCS or SMS through a provider we have a data processing agreement with.

## Missed calls

If you call and Eirik can't answer, your phone number and the time are stored, and you get a message with a link to the form. You won't get more than one such message within half an hour.

## No tracking and no cookies

The website does not use cookies, tracking scripts or storage in your browser. We only count requests and where they came from, on the server, and see overall visitor statistics from Cloudflare in which individuals cannot be identified.

## Service reminders

If you tick the box for reminders, we may send you a message when it is time for a service or seasonal work, such as winterising the cabin. The box is never ticked in advance. The legal basis is your consent, and you can withdraw it at any time by telling us.

## How long we keep it

- Requests that don't become a job, and their photos, are deleted no later than 12 months after the last contact.
- If it becomes a job, we keep the information as long as needed for complaints (up to five years) and for the accounts, as the Bookkeeping Act requires.
- Missed calls are deleted after three months.
- Consent to reminders is kept until you withdraw it.

## Your rights

You can ask to see what we have stored about you, have errors corrected, have information deleted, restrict or object to its use, and receive a copy of your information. Send an e-mail or call, and we will reply as soon as we can and within one month at the latest. If you believe we are handling your information in breach of the rules, you can complain to the Norwegian Data Protection Authority (Datatilsynet).

## Contact

Kobber og Kran AS is the data controller. Contact details and the organisation number are at the bottom of the page.
