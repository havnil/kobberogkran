Small businesses, housing co-ops and owners' associations also need a plumber who answers the phone and turns up when agreed. Eirik does service and repairs in buildings in Hemnes, Bjørkelangen, Aurskog and the rest of Aurskog-Høland.

## Typical jobs

- Leaks, blocked drains and dripping taps
- Replacing stopcocks and fitting water stop valves in the flats
- Replacing water heaters, with a fixed price on the work
- Laundry rooms, boiler rooms and other shared areas
- Kitchens and toilets in small businesses, shops and workshops

## For the board

- **One person to call:** Eirik is responsible for the job from start to finish
- **Group the jobs:** several flats on the same day means less driving
- **Documentation:** records and photos for the maintenance plan and the insurance

## How it works

1. **You send a request or call.** Choose “Business” in the form, and say what needs doing and how many units it covers.
2. **Eirik gives you a price.** Small jobs are charged at the hourly rate. Bigger jobs get an estimate or a fixed price.
3. **The job is done and documented.** You get an invoice with VAT shown and records of what was done.

## Partners

Jetting of main drains and full refurbishments of whole systems are done by regular partners. Eirik arranges the contact and follows the job through.
