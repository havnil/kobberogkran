There are many cabins in the forests around Hemnes, Setskog and Rømskog, and many of them stand cold for much of the winter. Frost damage at the cabin is often only discovered when the water is turned back on. Eirik helps you close it properly in autumn and open it properly in spring.

## Winterising in autumn

- The water is turned off, and pipes, water heater and taps are drained
- The toilet and traps are drained or filled with antifreeze
- The well, pump and pressure tank are protected if the cabin has them
- Eirik checks for visible leaks and notes what should be done before next season

## Opening in spring

- The water is turned on and air is bled from the pipes
- The water heater is filled before the power goes on
- All joints are checked for leaks and frost damage
- You get a message when the cabin is ready to use

## Frozen pipes

If something has frozen, turn off the main stopcock and switch off the power to the water heater. Don't use an open flame to thaw the pipes. Call Eirik and report it to your insurer. He finds the damage, repairs the pipes and helps you avoid it happening again, for example with heating cable or a better route for the pipe.

## What Eirik needs from you

- **Where the cabin is:** the address, “Use where I am now”, or the farm and title number
- **Access:** whether the road is cleared, any barrier or parking, and where the key is
- **Water:** mains water or a well, and whether the drain goes to a tank
- **Photos** of where the water comes in, the water heater and any damage
