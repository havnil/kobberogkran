Water for the animals can't wait until spring. There are many farms around Hemnes, Setskog and Rømskog, and many have their own well and long pipes out to the barn and pasture. Eirik helps with the water from the well or the mains all the way to the drinking bowl.

## What Eirik helps with

- **Barns and stables:** water pipes, drinking bowls and frost protection
- **Yard and pasture:** frost-free water posts and a pipe to where the water is needed
- **Milk room and wash bay:** hot water, mixer taps and drains
- **Well and pressure:** pump, pressure tank and filter when the farm has its own well
- **Frost:** thawing, repairs and heating cable on exposed pipes

## How it works

1. **You send a request or call.** Say what needs doing, where the water needs to go and whether the farm has a well.
2. **Eirik takes a look.** Small repairs are charged at the hourly rate. Bigger jobs get an estimate or a fixed price after a site visit.
3. **The work is done when it suits the farm.** You are told what was done, and what is worth checking before winter.

## What Eirik needs from you

- Where the water comes from: **a well or the mains**
- **Photos** of where the water enters the barn, and of the pump and pressure tank
- **Access** to the barn and which times suit the animals

## Partners

Well drilling, digging and electrical work are done by regular partners. Eirik arranges the contact and plans the job with them, or works with the ones you already use.
