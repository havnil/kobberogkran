Hydronic heating gives even warmth and can use cheaper energy, such as a heat pump or wood. Eirik installs new systems, replaces old ones and extends them when the house gets bigger. With a service agreement he checks the system every year, so it works when winter comes.

## What Eirik helps with

- **New system:** underfloor heating and radiators in new builds and renovations
- **Replacement:** circulation pump, valves, expansion vessel and manifold cabinet
- **Extension:** new circuits when the house is extended
- **Connection:** to a heat pump, electric boiler, wood boiler or district heating
- **Service:** bleeding, pressure and balancing, so every room gets warm

## Service agreement

A heating system that is checked every year lasts longer and uses less electricity. With a service agreement Eirik comes at a fixed time and goes over the system. You are told what should be fixed, and you do not have to remember it yourself.

## What Eirik needs from you

- **What kind of system** you have, or want
- **Photos** of the manifold cabinet, the pump and the heat source
- **What you notice:** cold rooms, noises, low pressure or a leak
- For a new system: a **drawing** or the room measurements

## Partners

Electrical work and fitting heat pumps are done by regular partners. Eirik arranges the contact and is responsible to you.
