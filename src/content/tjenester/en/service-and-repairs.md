Most of what a plumber does in an ordinary home is small things that become big if left alone: a dripping tap, a seal that has given up, a drain that gets a little slower every week. Eirik takes these jobs in Hemnes, Bjørkelangen, Aurskog and the rest of Aurskog-Høland.

## Typical jobs

- Dripping taps, and replacing mixer taps in kitchens and bathrooms
- Leaks under the sink, by the dishwasher or the washing machine
- Toilets that keep running, flush poorly or need replacing
- Blocked sinks, shower drains or toilets
- Stopcocks that won't close, and replacing the main stopcock
- Fitting dishwashers, washing machines and outdoor taps
- Frost-free outdoor taps, so the garden hose doesn't burst the pipe in winter

## How it works

1. **You send a request or call.** A photo of the tap, drain or leak saves time. The make of the tap or toilet helps Eirik bring the right part.
2. **Eirik gives you a price.** Small jobs are charged at the hourly rate, and you know before he starts. For larger jobs you get an estimate.
3. **The job gets done.** Eirik finds the cause, not just the symptom, and tells you if he sees anything else worth keeping an eye on.

## What you can do before Eirik arrives

- Know where the main stopcock is, and check that it closes.
- Clear the space under the sink or around whatever needs fixing.
- Collect the small jobs, so they can be done on the same visit.

## What we don't do

Electrical work and jetting of main drains are done by others. If the job needs it, Eirik will tell you and help you on your way.
