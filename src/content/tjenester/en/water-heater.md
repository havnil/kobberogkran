A leaking water heater is one of the most common causes of water damage in Norwegian homes. If your tank is old, it is cheaper to replace it before it leaks than after. Kobber og Kran replaces water heaters at a **fixed price for the work** when the new tank goes in the same place as the old one.

## A fixed price, but for the work only

The fixed price covers what is the same from job to job: draining and disconnecting the old tank, fitting the new one, connecting the water, pressure testing and taking the old one away. **The tank itself and parts are extra.** That is why we also show a typical total, so you can see what a replacement usually costs all in.

If something isn't as it should be, for example the tank has to be moved or there is no floor drain, you are told, with the price, before Eirik starts.

## What Eirik needs from you

- **A photo of the rating plate** on the tank. It shows size, power and age.
- **A photo of the tank and the floor around it**, so he can see the drain, any leak stop and the space.
- **Which room and which floor** the tank is on.
- **Whether the tank is plugged into a socket** or hard-wired.

You can add all of this in the form at the bottom of the page. In most cases Eirik can then give you a price without a site visit.

## How the replacement goes

1. Eirik turns off the water and the power to the tank, and drains the old one.
2. The old tank is disconnected and carried out.
3. The new one goes in with a safety valve and a mixing valve.
4. The water is connected, and everything is pressure tested and checked for leaks.
5. You get FDV documentation for the new tank, and the old one is taken away.

## When should the tank be replaced?

- It is around 20 years old or more.
- There is rust or damp around the connections or under the tank.
- The hot water runs out faster than before, or the water comes out rusty.
- The safety valve drips all the time.
